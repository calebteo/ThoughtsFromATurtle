<strong>Legacy:</strong> I recalled an idea that was hatched between me and a good friend. It was called Project "God's Glory" (as a placeholder until we got a better name). The goal was to write and share reflections. To sharpen our minds through writing and formulating concrete and deeper thoughts. 
I was not convinced of the need for the idea all those years ago but now I have learnt. Many missed years but we must move and learn.
